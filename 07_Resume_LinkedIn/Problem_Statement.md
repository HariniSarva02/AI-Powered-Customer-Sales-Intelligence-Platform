Problem Statement
Customer & Sales Intelligence Platform
1. Business Problem
Businesses generate large amounts of sales data containing information about customers, products, orders, sales, profits, discounts, and returns. However, raw sales data alone does not provide clear insights into business performance.

Without proper data analysis and visualization, businesses may struggle to identify:
- Which products and categories generate the highest sales and profits.
- Which regions and customer segments perform best.
- How sales and profits change over time.
- Which products experience losses despite generating high sales.
- How customer purchasing behavior affects business performance.
- How product returns affect overall sales performance.
As a result, businesses may find it difficult to identify performance gaps, understand customer behavior, and make informed business decisions.
2. Project Need
There is a need for a centralized analytics platform that can transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights through data cleaning, analysis, visualization, and reporting.

The platform should help users understand sales performance, profitability, customer behavior, product performance, regional trends, and returns using interactive dashboards.
3. Proposed Solution
The Customer & Sales Intelligence Platform is developed to analyze retail sales data and provide actionable business insights.

The project uses Python, Pandas, SQL, and Streamlit to process, analyze, and visualize sales information.

The platform aims to:
1. Clean and validate raw sales data.
2. Analyze sales, revenue, profit, and customer performance.
3. Identify top-performing and loss-making products.
4. Evaluate regional and customer segment performance.
5. Analyze product returns and their impact.
6. Present insights through interactive dashboards.
4. Expected Business Value
The platform helps businesses:
- Monitor overall sales and profitability.
- Identify high-performing products and regions.
- Understand customer and segment performance.
- Detect potential business problems.
- Make data-driven decisions.
- Improve business performance through better visibility into sales data.
